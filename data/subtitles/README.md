Drop your `.srt` files here (up to 100). Name them `Title (Year).srt`
(e.g. `Inception (2010).srt`) so titles and years are extracted cleanly;
scene-release style names such as `The.Dark.Knight.2008.1080p.BluRay.srt` also work.

Subtitle files are not committed (see `.gitignore`).
Then run: `movie-ingest --subtitles data/subtitles`
